Summer School "Elasticity and Growth"

Mechanobiology, nonlinear elasticity, and growth instabilities

May 11-14, 2026 Aula Manfredi Romano, Departement of Structure for Engeneering and Architecture

Overview

Three main lecture series (8 hours each) and two invited junior one-hour seminars.

Topics include growth in living matter, nonlinear elasticity, and mechanobiology.
